A Place to Learn, Explore & Belong

More Than a
Kids Club

Welcome to Kinderbay — a thoughtfully designed kids club at Millennium Atria Hotel, Business Bay, where children can learn, explore, play and build meaningful friendships in a safe and inspiring environment.

From early learning and creative activities to movement, music, events and everyday adventures, Kinderbay gives children a place to grow at their own pace while giving parents peace of mind.

The space

Six little bays to explore

Half day · Membership

Circle Time & School Readiness

A teacher-led, half-day membership program of circle time and school-readiness learning — preschool structure, Kinderbay warmth.

Active · outdoor

Outdoor Jungle Gym

Open-air climbing frames, rope bridges and monkey bars to build balance, strength and big-kid confidence.

Active play

Indoor Slides

Soft twisting slides and padded landings — endless giggles, completely safe for little climbers.

Imaginative play

Toy Library

A whole library of blocks, cars, dolls and pretend-play sets to spark every kind of story.

Screens & board games

Video Game Room

A supervised zone of kid-friendly consoles, interactive games and board games for the older kids.

Calm corner

Library Tree

A cosy storybook nook with cushioned seating beneath our reading tree — the perfect spot to wind down quietly.

Designed With Little Ones in Mind

A community for children & parents

Friendships Start Here.

Kinderbay isn't only about keeping children busy — it's about creating a community. Children meet others, play together, learn social skills and develop friendships in a welcoming environment. Parents become part of it too, connected through our private WhatsApp group, events and shared experiences.

Because childhood is better when it's shared.

Always know what they're up to

Stay Connected, Even When You're Away

We believe parents shouldn't have to wonder what their children are doing all day. Kinderbay gives parents direct access to our cameras, so you can check in on your little ones anytime.

We also share daily photos and updates in our private parents' WhatsApp group — a glimpse into the activities, smiles, friendships and little achievements that happen throughout the day.

They're having their adventure. You're still part of it.
